Data sources & map notes

DMV: California DMV office locations. Boundary: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb, county GEOID 06037. Points are selected by intersection with the actual county polygon, including offshore islands. This inventory includes specialized offices and is not a complete directory of kiosks or business partners. It does not provide live wait times, operating status, or service availability. Data is requested on page load; source updates may change the count. Educational project; not an official DMV application. Built with the Esri Intro to FeatureLayer sample and ArcGIS Maps SDK for JavaScript 5.1.
